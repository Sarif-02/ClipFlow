# Code of conduct

We want participation in ClipFlow to be respectful and constructive. Welcome people with different backgrounds and levels of experience, discuss decisions on their merits, credit others' work, and respect reasonable boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexual harassment, sustained disruption, and sharing someone else's private information without permission are unacceptable in project spaces. Project spaces include issues, pull requests, discussions, and interactions where someone is acting on behalf of ClipFlow.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through an available private channel. Include enough context for maintainers to review the incident and keep personal information out of public issues. For software vulnerabilities, use the [security policy](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may remove content, warn participants, or restrict participation in response to harmful behavior. They should consider the severity, protect reporters' privacy, and explain actions to affected participants when appropriate.
